Dawon Park박다원

InnoCORE Fellow · KAIST

I build high-fidelity numerical models of structures under extreme loads — blast, debris flow, earthquake — and work on making those models calibrate themselves. Most of my work lives where finite element analysis, material modeling and machine learning meet.

  • finite element analysis
  • blast & impact simulation
  • material modeling
  • SPH–FEM coupling
  • fragility assessment
  • surrogate modeling
